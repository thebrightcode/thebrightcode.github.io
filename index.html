About

I learn by building — then making it better.

It started in Class 9 with a gaming YouTube channel. To make the videos better, I taught myself video editing — which pulled me into thumbnails, banners, logos and content creation along the way. I kept experimenting from there: lyric edits, nature clips, trending formats, growing that channel to around 2,000 subscribers.

Eventually I moved into Instagram theme pages, learning what actually moves an audience — content selection, consistency, real growth instead of guesswork.

Two theme pages, run at the same time, grew to roughly 22,000 and 24,000 followers — no shortcuts, no fake growth.

That same patience is what pulled me into frontend development — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress and AI-assisted building — while I keep sharpening SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads on the side.

I'm not claiming to be an expert at all of it. I'm someone who learns by building — and I'd rather be upfront about where I am than pretend otherwise.

What I Do

Here's exactly what I can help with.

Frontend Websites

Responsive websites built around your content, brand and goals — coded by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript rather than assembled from page-builder templates. That means cleaner markup, faster load times, and a site that's easier to maintain or extend later, without the extra weight most template-based builders carry.

WordPress Websites

A WordPress site built on clean, lightweight code rather than a stack of heavy plugins — set up so you can update text, swap images and add pages yourself after launch, without calling me for every small change. The structure stays simple, so it's easy to hand off and maintain.

Landing Pages with AI

A single page built to do one job well — turn a visitor into a lead or a sale. I write the structure and code by hand, using AI to move faster on layout options and copy drafts, then build around a clear hierarchy and one focused call-to-action.

Graphic Design

Logo, banner, thumbnail and poster design — each built around how it will actually be used, not just how it looks alone. I think about scale, contrast and legibility from the start, so a logo reads clearly at icon size and a thumbnail still grabs attention next to others.

Video Editing

Editing for YouTube, Instagram and short-form content, from raw footage to a finished, publish-ready cut. I focus on pacing and clarity — cutting dead space, syncing sound and visuals cleanly — so the final edit holds attention without feeling over-produced or generic.

Process

What happens after you reach out.

A simple, honest sequence — no unnecessary steps in between.
